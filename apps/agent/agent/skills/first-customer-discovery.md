# First customer discovery

Treat this as public-signal lead supply, not list scraping.

1. Read the requested count and markets from the task reason.
2. Search several distinct buckets: current landscaping hiring, operations growth, estimator or project-manager hiring, fragmented field-to-office workflows, and explicit process/tool pain.
3. Prefer an official company page or current official job posting. A directory listing or search snippet is not enough on its own.
4. Keep only landscaping, design-build, outdoor-living or closely adjacent operators with a specific current signal that Lode could solve.
5. Exclude agencies, software vendors, directories, inactive businesses, duplicate domains and companies outside the requested markets.
6. Use only public business information. Do not collect private addresses, personal emails, personal phone numbers or protected traits.
7. Call `record_discovered_prospects` once with the retained companies. Its candidates become research tasks; they are not qualified or send-ready yet.
8. Never send outreach or submit a form.

For every retained candidate, state the observed public signal separately from why it creates urgency. Use country code `GB` for the United Kingdom.
