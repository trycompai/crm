# Data-Gear GPU Buyer Prospecting — Source of Truth

Last updated: 2026-09-23

## Proven signal

The YOLO Auto outreach produced an immediate request for pricing on a complete 8x NVIDIA B300 HGX server. Treat this as a validated buyer-profile signal for Data-Gear prospecting.

## Primary prospect profile

Prioritize companies that operate or are actively building their own GPU-backed AI infrastructure, especially:

- inference API and model-serving companies
- AI platforms offering dedicated/private GPU capacity
- agent infrastructure companies with sustained inference workloads
- private-AI / on-prem AI platforms
- smaller GPU cloud and bare-metal GPU providers
- recently funded AI startups with evidence of self-hosting, dedicated capacity, cluster build-out, or cloud-cost/privacy pressure
- companies discussing model serving, concurrency, throughput, dedicated clusters, NVLink/NVSwitch, H100/H200/B200/B300, HGX/DGX, InfiniBand/RoCE, or capacity expansion

Do not limit prospecting to companies that sell hardware. The stronger signal is evidence that the company consumes significant GPU compute.

## Qualification order

1. Direct public evidence of dedicated GPU clusters, self-hosted inference, private infrastructure, bare metal, or owned/leased model-serving capacity.
2. Public engineering/job/funding signal implying near-term GPU capacity growth.
3. Recent funding or revenue growth sufficient to support six-figure infrastructure purchases.
4. A public and verifiable business contact channel.
5. Dedupe against Data-Gear CRM and sent-email history before outreach.

## Negative filters

Deprioritize:

- generic AI startups with no evidence of meaningful compute demand
- companies that are purely wrappers around third-party APIs
- hyperscalers and very large buyers whose procurement is unlikely to use a specialist reseller
- companies already contacted unless a new buying trigger exists
- guessed or inferred personal email addresses when a verified business address is available

## Outreach positioning

Lead with Data-Gear as a source for configured GPU servers and AI infrastructure. Use datacenter terminology and concrete platform language. Avoid generic AI-sales copy.

Relevant product language may include:

- NVIDIA HGX / DGX-class systems
- 8x B200/B300 or current-generation SXM GPU servers
- AMD EPYC / Intel Xeon
- high-capacity DDR5 ECC
- NVMe storage
- ConnectX-7 / ConnectX-8
- InfiniBand / high-speed Ethernet
- rack-ready air- or liquid-cooled systems
- availability, lead time, warranty, and freight

## Prospecting loop

Research -> verify infrastructure signal -> verify contact -> dedupe -> create CRM prospect -> outreach -> record sent status -> watch for buying trigger/reply.

When a reply contains a hardware requirement, convert immediately from generic outreach to RFQ handling and obtain distributor/OEM buy pricing before quoting the customer.

## Reference case

YOLO Auto is the reference case for this profile: a prospect with dedicated/model-serving GPU infrastructure that responded to Data-Gear outreach with an immediate request for an 8x B300 HGX server quote.

This document is the source of truth for the Data-Gear GPU-buyer prospecting profile until superseded by a later documented rule.
